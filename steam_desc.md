Expanded Field Weapons (EFW) expands Hearts of Iron IV with new field equipment, improvised weapons, emergency vehicles and national economic mechanics while keeping the vanilla systems at the center of the experience.

The mod introduces dedicated production for machine guns, mortars and infantry anti-tank weapons, together with new support companies and a Heavy Weapons battalion.

It also introduces desperate, low-cost solutions for countries with limited industrial capacity—including the **ultra-cheap, zero-resource Pipe-Gun Rifle**, improvised naval vessels, a wooden fighter aircraft and an inexpensive improvised light tank.

A new strategic development system introduces Propaganda Centers, Labour Directorates, Research Centers and Logistics Centers. These buildings are connected to decisions for National Investments, economic coordination and full National Mobilization.

Early-game economic decisions allow countries to accelerate industrialization through public works, state subsidies, infrastructure programs and national bonds, exchanging Political Power, manpower, trains or future costs for immediate development.

### Main features:

- **EFW Pipe-Gun Rifle** – Ultra-cheap, zero-resource improvised rifle designed for mass garrisoning, desperate last stands, and minor nations
- Machine Guns, Mortars and Infantry Anti-Tank Weapons as separate equipment
- New support companies
- Heavy Weapons line battalion
- Integration with vanilla Support Weapons technologies
- Improvised naval designs
- EFW Wooden Fighter
- EFW Tin Tank
- Four strategic national buildings
- National Investments and National Mobilization progression
- National Bonds and long-term loans
- Early-game industrialization and public works decisions
- Designed primarily for HOI4 1.19.x

---

### NOTE !!!

Some EFW content is initialized through scripted effects and may take 1–2 in-game days to appear after starting a new game.

- **Pipe-Gun Rifle:** Available immediately from day one to quickly equip second-line units, ports, and frontline placeholders without consuming strategic resources.
- **Heavy Field Weapons:** For Machine Guns, Mortars, and Infantry Anti-Tank production, make sure **Support Weapons I** has been researched.

After the required technology is available, allow the game to run for approximately 1–2 in-game days so the mod can initialize the related equipment and variants.

Some improvised vehicles and designer-based equipment may also require the relevant vanilla designer/system to be available.

The mod is still under active development and balance values may change based on testing and feedback.\_
