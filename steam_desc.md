Expanded Field Weapons (EFW) expands Hearts of Iron IV with new field equipment, improvised weapons, emergency vehicles and national economic mechanics while keeping the vanilla systems at the center of the experience.

The mod introduces dedicated production for machine guns, mortars and infantry anti-tank weapons, together with new support companies and a Heavy Weapons battalion.

It also introduces desperate, low-cost solutions for countries with limited industrial capacity—including the **ultra-cheap, zero-resource Pipe-Gun Rifle**, improvised naval vessels and a wooden fighter aircraft, each with its own set of improvised designer modules (pressed-paper drop tanks, "cast-iron stove" armour, sails, careened hulls and more).

A new strategic development system introduces Propaganda Centers, Labour Directorates, Research Centers and Logistics Centers. These buildings are connected to decisions for economic coordination and full National Mobilization.

Seven resource buildings (steel, oil, rubber, aluminium, tungsten, chromium and coal) let you develop raw materials directly in your states without using building slots. Public Housing increases local manpower and recruitable population, while Wheat Fields reduce consumer goods and open new building slots. The state view has been enlarged so that all buildings remain visible.

Early-game economic decisions allow countries to accelerate industrialization through two-phase industrial plans, grand public works, an emergency naval programme, fuel autarky, a social pact and national bonds, exchanging Political Power, manpower, trains, trucks or future costs for immediate development.

**New laws in the politics panel:** four new sections—Economy and Finance, Society and Internal Order, Army and General Staff, Navy, Air Force and Services—with 6 law slots each and 8 laws per slot (192 laws). In every slot five laws cost 50 PP and three slightly stronger ones cost 100 PP. Every law mixes bonuses and drawbacks: none is free, but the right combination gives a real edge. Slots start empty and the AI does not use these laws.

**National Vocations:** pick ONE path (Republic of Scientists, Workshop of the Nation, Nation in Arms, Lords of the Sea or the Art of Making Do) and push it to tier three. Every tier needs EFW buildings and matching laws: line them up and you get huge bonuses (up to +40% research from the vocation alone), paid for with drawbacks in the other fields.

**Welcome event:** a ministry inspector with a hydraulic pipe introduces the mod and offers a starting gift.

**Built-in FPS optimisation:** lighter map graphics (no shadows, trees, water refraction, civilian 3D buildings or 3D weather particles), shorter logs and less frequent AI recalculation. EFW's own scripts have no daily checks: technology sync runs weekly and switches itself off once a country has unlocked everything. Ground snow and every other weather effect on combat are unchanged.

**Languages:** English, Russian, German, Polish and Simplified Chinese (plus Italian for players using an Italian translation mod).

### NOTE !!!

The wooden fighter and the improvised ships appear 1–2 in-game days after starting a game.

- **Pipe-Gun Rifle:** Available immediately from day one to quickly equip second-line units, ports, and frontline placeholders without consuming strategic resources.
- **Heavy Field Weapons:** For Machine Guns, Mortars, and Infantry Anti-Tank production, make sure **Support Weapons I** has been researched.

After the required technology is researched, the new equipment unlocks within one in-game week.

Some improvised vehicles and designer-based equipment may also require the relevant vanilla designer/system to be available.

### Compatibility

EFW replaces the vanilla state view (`countrystateview.gui`), the building icon strip and its sprite definition. Mods that change the state view or the building icons are not compatible with this part of EFW.

EFW also replaces the politics-panel section icons (`GFX_idea_categories`), the weather file (`common/weather.txt`) and adds its own graphics/AI defines. Mods that add their own law sections or change these files may conflict.

The Rapid Invasions option uses country modifiers instead of defines, so it only applies to the player who enables it.

The politics-panel law icons come from the free **Ultimate HOI4 GFX** pack (github.com/Globvs/Ultimate-HOI4-GFX).
