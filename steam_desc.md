Expanded Field Weapons (EFW) expands Hearts of Iron IV with new field equipment, improvised weapons, emergency vehicles and national economic mechanics while keeping the vanilla systems at the center of the experience.

The mod introduces dedicated production for machine guns, mortars and infantry anti-tank weapons, together with new support companies and a Heavy Weapons battalion.

It also introduces desperate, low-cost solutions for countries with limited industrial capacity—including the **ultra-cheap, zero-resource Pipe-Gun Rifle**, improvised naval vessels and a wooden fighter aircraft, each with its own set of improvised designer modules (pressed-paper drop tanks, "cast-iron stove" armour, sails, careened hulls and more).

A new strategic development system introduces Propaganda Centers, Labour Directorates, Research Centers and Logistics Centers. These buildings are connected to decisions for economic coordination and full National Mobilization.

Seven resource buildings (steel, oil, rubber, aluminium, tungsten, chromium and coal) let you develop raw materials directly in your states without using building slots. Public Housing increases local manpower and recruitable population, while Wheat Fields reduce consumer goods and open new building slots. The state view has been enlarged so that all buildings remain visible.

Early-game economic decisions allow countries to accelerate industrialization through two-phase industrial plans, grand public works, an emergency naval programme, fuel autarky, a social pact and national bonds, exchanging Political Power, manpower, trains, trucks or future costs for immediate development.

**New laws in the politics panel:** four new sections—Economy and Finance, Society and Internal Order, Army and General Staff, Navy, Air Force and Services—with 6 law slots each and 8 laws per slot (192 laws). In every slot five laws cost 50 PP and three slightly stronger ones cost 100 PP. Every law mixes bonuses and drawbacks: none is free, but the right combination gives a real edge. Slots start empty and the AI does not use these laws.

**Built-in FPS optimisation:** lighter map graphics (no shadows, trees, water refraction, civilian 3D buildings or 3D weather particles), shorter logs and less frequent AI recalculation. Ground snow and every other weather effect on combat are unchanged.

### Main features:

- **EFW Pipe-Gun Rifle** – Ultra-cheap, zero-resource improvised rifle designed for mass garrisoning, desperate last stands, and minor nations
- Machine Guns, Mortars and Infantry Anti-Tank Weapons as separate equipment
- New support companies
- Heavy Weapons line battalion
- Integration with vanilla Support Weapons technologies
- Improvised naval designs and modules
- EFW Wooden Fighter and improvised aircraft modules
- Custom icons for every EFW unit and designer module
- Four strategic national buildings
- Seven resource buildings, one per resource (no building slots)
- Public Housing and Wheat Fields
- Enlarged state view showing up to 15 buildings without slots
- 4 new politics-panel sections: 24 law slots, 192 laws
- 21 generic designers available to every country: tank, naval, aircraft, materiel and industrial concerns
- 8 generic political advisors: one government advisor per ideology (democratic, communist, fascist, non-aligned) and four technical advisors
- Optional Rapid Invasions, switchable on and off with a decision
- Economic coordination and National Mobilization progression
- Early-game industrial plans, public works, naval programme and national bonds
- Built-in FPS optimisation
- Designed primarily for HOI4 1.19.x

### NOTE !!!

Some EFW content is initialized through scripted effects and may take 1–2 in-game days to appear after starting a new game.

- **Pipe-Gun Rifle:** Available immediately from day one to quickly equip second-line units, ports, and frontline placeholders without consuming strategic resources.
- **Heavy Field Weapons:** For Machine Guns, Mortars, and Infantry Anti-Tank production, make sure **Support Weapons I** has been researched.

After the required technology is available, allow the game to run for approximately 1–2 in-game days so the mod can initialize the related equipment and variants.

Some improvised vehicles and designer-based equipment may also require the relevant vanilla designer/system to be available.

### Compatibility

EFW replaces the vanilla state view (`countrystateview.gui`), the building icon strip and its sprite definition. Mods that change the state view or the building icons (for example Resource Plants) are not compatible with this part of EFW.

EFW also replaces the politics-panel section icons (`GFX_idea_categories`), the weather file (`common/weather.txt`) and adds its own graphics/AI defines. Mods that add their own law sections (for example Better Laws and Policies) or change these files may conflict.

The resource buildings are based on the **Resource Plants** mod (Workshop 3513657215).

The Rapid Invasions option is based on the **Invasions** mod (Workshop 3709777606). It uses country modifiers instead of defines, so it only applies to the player who enables it.

The politics-panel laws are inspired by **Better Laws and Policies** (Workshop 3097661966) and reuse its law icons.

The generic designers are based on **Designers Extend** (Workshop 2742251002), updated to the 1.19 airframes. Disable the original mod when using EFW.

The FPS optimisation is based on **FPS BOOSTER** (Workshop 3765424442), corrected for the 1.19 define tables.
